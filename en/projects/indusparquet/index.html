The project developed for Indus Parquet, a Brazilian company specialising in the production and installation of timber flooring with more than 80 years of experience, emerged as an architectural interpretation of the values of sustainability, traceability and connection with nature that define the brand. Located in the Nunez neighbourhood, the flagship store offers an immersive experience in which wood is presented in its purest and most essential state. The project draws inspiration from the controlled planting and harvesting processes the company carries out in different regions of Brazil, incorporating a spatial narrative connected to the forest landscape and the organic origin of the raw material.

The architectural proposal is structured around an orthogonal composition of exposed natural logs, used as facade elements, cladding and to shape the topography of the outdoor entrance. This approach plane acts as a sculptural surface that organises the relationship with the public realm and creates both a physical and perceptual threshold from the pavement. Inside, the space unfolds through a winding, non-linear route inspired by the experience of moving through a forest, connecting areas for display, customer service and material exploration.

At the centre of the project is a series of floor-to-ceiling pivoting structures displaying more than forty varieties of wood and finishes. A creative table provides a place for architects, clients and brand advisers to work together. The route culminates on an upper level intended for meetings, events and gastronomic experiences, integrating a landscaped terrace, designer furniture, signature pieces, specialised lighting and artworks. Together, these elements consolidate a complete brand experience in which architecture, landscape, art and materiality operate as a single narrative system.
