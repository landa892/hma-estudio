International competition for Accor: identity and community as programme, not decoration.

It is impossible to ignore that hotels today base their proposals on themed aesthetics and social activities designed for their guests' enjoyment, and this is an undeniable advantage over short-term accommodation such as Airbnb. So why is Airbnb a threat to the hotel business? Or why do most hotels base their proposals on themed or story-driven concepts? Today, Airbnb is like living at home. Hotels, by contrast, are places of transit: despite their renewed thematic proposals, they dress and undress according to fashion, without a clear identity. This reveals a lack of identity.

Our proposal can be summed up in two words: identity and community, both addressed as part of the hotel's operation and entertainment. Identity is an integration of concepts that define an individual or an object, but the hotel should not have a fixed identity; rather, it should serve as a container. A large framework that each guest can fill with their belongings, just as they would at home. A truly tailored offer.

The composition must follow a coherent system that gives it unity. We do not conceive interior architecture as a collection of individual elements: decorative objects, furniture and lighting. Our proposal is defined by a broad group of pieces that form a space in themselves. This allows multiple possible configurations for use in new hotels or in existing hotels undergoing renovation.

Together, these pieces form a living organism that is versatile and adaptable to each key space in the hotel, shaping and organising everything from lighting to fixed and movable equipment. In addition to its organisational role, the system also has a symbolic one: through its old suitcases, it holds the personal objects of every traveller who passes through the hotel. The stamps on those old suitcases - evidence of journeys through different states and provinces - become part of the local identity expressed in a wallpaper. Through visual communication, we emphasise the citizens' shared local identity with a large video wall at the front desk. We also propose the active presence of the most representative local flora. All of these elements are brought together in a single connecting construction system.

It is a void, defined as such by a pure and simple aesthetic. It is free of imposed styling, although its lines undoubtedly construct a universe associated with the modern rejection of eclecticism and ornament, and with the status of the contemporary individual. Today, that restraint is called minimalism: life with only what is essential and necessary. Something truly timeless. The minimum invites us to reflect on a way of life based on limited, measured, basic and sustainable consumption.

The contemporary traveller knows that a carry-on can hold everything they need. We believe that today a camera, a laptop for writing, a book for reading or a notebook for drawing is undoubtedly more valuable than two suitcases full of clothes. These are the objects that define part of our identity. The value of the emotional over the material. Identity is universal: it is the aspect that defines us.
