BEACH RESTAURANT

Our analysis is based on an organizational and representative guiding idea. Organizational because it defines the layout where the hierarchies and locations of the sitting system are resolved. Representative because we understand that the material must be a tectonic and poetic condition to generate the determining DNA of the project. The sugar canes whose production is of marked influence in Central America is the poetic language whose technical resolution we propose in bamboo canes, which offers a lot of constructive versatility. Its particularity allows us to understand it as a design element. The formal resolution, we propose it through a construction of waves that emulate the waters of the ocean. The indivisible link between the logical definition of a beach restaurant and the collective imaginary of waves. The cladding, flooring, furniture and decoration are resolved with materials and colors that refer to nature, the sea and its connection with both elements. Other decisions, such as the use of ropes and nets that give character to architectural and decorative elements, are part of this world that connects this sector with its immediate surroundings.

LOBBY

The conceptual design is rooted in an aesthetic inspired by the typical pitched roofs of Caribbean homes, preserving all their components, from beams and trusses to wooden shingles and rafters, right down to the tiles and skylights. Two spaces housing a bar and reception were crafted while maintain the weathered roof tile materials and greenery, as if they were part of an authentic Caribbean home where the passage of time has seamlessly integrated nature with architecture. Shutters are employed to functionally divide both side entrances, further emphasizing the symbolic language woven throughout the entire space. Furniture selection has been carefully curated to harmonize the hotel with its natural environment. The presence of fixtures inspired by local fauna and the marine ambiance, featured in both lighting and decor, heightens the feeling of connectivity with the surrounding nature.

CORRIDORS

These spaces function as pathways connecting the 5 buildings. Our design approach has centered around neutral and understated materials, focusing on subtle changes in texture rather than color shifts. We have preserved the presence of natural, matte wood tones, along with textured light grays. Hints of green emerge through carefully curated vegetation, which included preserved moss wall and native plants. A striking suspended structure, reminiscent of the commercial boats found in Barbado´s port, stretches across the entire length of these circular areas. The artisanal and vernacular design of the furniture maintains the overall style while emphasizing a deep reverence for tradition.

PREFERRED RECEPTION

This prominent and sophisticated space within the complex should be showcased as a preferential and hierarchical area. Therefore, we find in vernacular art and culture a communication channel to represent the unique and detailed aesthetics of this complex area. It is a space that invites us to immerse ourselves in the tradition of Barbados, from its role as a commercial port in the Americas, while also acknowledging its pre-Columbian origins. A suspended structure that emulates ancient commercial boats, along with the presence of stacked rocks as a nod to pre-Columbian origins. A suspended structure that emulates ancient commercial boats, along with the presence of stacked rocks as a nod to pre-Columbian culture, both serve as major focal points. Details such as an interior oasis of native vegetation, combined with organic furniture and materials like cane, contribute to the integration of vernacular aesthetics.

LOBBY BAR

Its design language forms an integral part of the central lobby, mirroring the approach taken in the coffee lounge area. Its functional structure seamlessly blends with the overall space, with a distinct orientation towards the pool and the beach. Roof tiles, coral stone, and lush vegetation are the dominant elements that contribute to crafting this environment where nature harmoniously coexists with the bar area, serving as an organizing element. This space, characterized by the size of the roof tiles, both modulates and provides purpose to the bottle area. The furniture, once again, evokes a strong connection with the natural surroundings.

COFFEE LOUNGE

This area constitutes an annex to the coffee space, yet it is seamlessly integrated with the hotel´s common areas. Given this, the aesthetics should harmoniously blend with the hotel´s shared spaces. As a strategic choice, we have incorporated a hanging representation of reinterpretations of boats in the circulation area, mirroring what we´ve done in the preferred reception. The central area of the coffee lounge is covered by a substantial wooden pergola and shaded by parasols that resemble shutters. This space´s functionality is intertwined with the coffee shop and prioritizes the views, so the seating layout must be highly adaptable and, above all, playful. Materials such as weathered rooftiles create visually striking corners that are perfect for social media snapshots. Finally, the greenery and paving materials simulate stones and cobblestones, enhancing the overall ambiance.

COFFEE SHOP

The café pays tribute to the historical tradition of the cobblestone streets of the colonial city through its most evocative symbol: the shutters of Caribbean homes. Ceramic brick, another characteristic element, serves as cladding in front of the counter and suggest a more contemporary language. In contrast to the traditional style of shutters, a prominent elliptical element, simple and modern in design, stands out as a central piece in both lighting and decoration. This fusion of the classic and the contemporary reflects the duality between the past and the future that defines this place. The colors chosen in its materials, such as browns, ochres, and natural tones, blend harmoniously with the coffee color, creating integration though tones and textures.

BUFFET

The space features the iconic presence of sugar cane as a prominent and standout element. It is used both in its simple form and intricately worked and cut to create alternative and functional uses. Sugar cane, whose production significantly influences Central America, serves as the poetical language for all the key elements within this space. We propose its technical resolution using bamboo canes, which offer considerable construction versatility. The inclusion of native vegetation and green walls inside, along with the use of coral cladding, come together to create a space that embodies as a strong connection to nature.

ASIAN RESTAURANT

We have drawn inspiration from certain Asian philosophical concepts, such as the beauty of imperfection, the appreciation of the incomplete, and subtle refinement that doesn´t flaunt itself, speak, or show off, but rather hints at it. These concepts have been translated into our space. The upper cladding, which modulates voids and solids, emphasizes “the incomplete” and is constructed using wood treated with the “Yakisugi” method. The soft pink tones directly relate to the subtle refinement that doesn´t boast. The large lamps have been reimagined as furniture pieces. We have incorporated recognizable elements and symbols from everyday Asian culture, such as Oriental lanterns reimagined as prominent furniture pieces, kakemonos, and Noreen curtains, all contributing to the presence of Asian culture in our restaurant. We have focused on three significant objects that emulate Oriental lanterns and serve as the focal points: the show cooking areas. Lastly, an element at the entrance mimics an artistically composed archeological piece.

BEACH RESTAURANT

The beach restaurant offers a nuanced perspective of the Caribbean coastal region’s natural beauty. The cladding, flooring, furniture, and decor incorporate materials and colors that evoke the essence of nature and the sea, establishing a profound connection with both elements. Additional design choices, such as the incorporation of nautical ropes and fishing nets, infuse character into architectural and decorative components, seamlessly integrating into the environment that surrounds this space. Our formal design solutions centers around the creation of wave-like structures that emulate the ocean´s rhythmic patterns. These waves are crafted from bamboo wood, chosen for its construction versatility and its cultural significance in this region.

ITALIAN RESTAURANT

Italy has wielded two significant influences throughout ancient and recent history: first, through the existence of the Roman Empire, and more recently, though its cultural and culinary impact following World War II. Arches and domes held paramount importance in the ancient world until the Renaissance period. Both elements take center stage in this space. The domes, in the midst of their construction process and surrounded by scaffolding, conjure the notion of a possible atelier reminiscent of Leonardo Da Vinci´s. The entire ambiance of the space ins imbued with a materiality that draws inspiration from the aesthetic of romantic Italy: brick, limestone ceramics, wooden structures and textiles. The architectural presence of Italian neorealism, paired with mecano-style shelving and furnishings, offers a contemporary language that reflects a more present-day Italy.

SPORT BAR

Barbados is rich in culture, history, gastronomy and sports. The sports bar is inspired by its iconic drink, rum, and the national sport: cricket. These two elements combined inspire us to create z gathering place that invites people to explore the island´s history and generates a sense of enjoyment and fun. The old barrels and stills of its distilleries, where this national drink is prepared, inspire the colors, textures, and patterns of the space´s cladding. The presence of historical, informative, and advertising images of rum helps those who visit understand the importance of this beverage for the island.

POOL BAR

The essence of the beach, encapsulated in the sand, the language of corals, and the maritime breeze, has been our guiding inspiration for this space. Given its elevated location within the building, we have embraced the winds as an integral part of the experience by incorporating long fabrics hanging from the ceiling. Sandy tones define the aesthetics of the furniture and its textures, while the coral motif is elegantly brought to life through perforated metal sheets that take center stage by adorning the bar´s backdrop. In certain design elements, we have infused an aesthetic reminiscent of the glamour and style of vintage-era swimmers. This purposeful design choice accentuates the presence of the adjacent pool, enhanced by a bar that offers a direct view, creating a seamless connection between the two spaces.
